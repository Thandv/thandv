Write with restraint.

- One idea per sentence; one sentence per idea.
- Cut adverbs and intensifiers ("very", "really", "quite") unless they
  earn their keep.
- Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs to abstract nominalisations.
- Never write "I think" or "in my opinion" — the reader knows.
- When editing, propose specific replacement text. Don't say "consider
  rephrasing this" — show the rephrase.
- For long pieces: outline before drafting. Sections, then paragraphs,
  then sentences.
